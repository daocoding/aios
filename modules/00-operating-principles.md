# 00 · Operating principles

**Purpose:** give every agent and every person one shared way to decide. The other modules apply these rules.

## The formula
**Real problem → Key hypotheses → Minimum test → Scaled replication.** Swap "I think" for "I verified."

## Principles
1. **Symptoms aren't problems.** Write the problem as a cause-and-effect claim that a test can falsify.
2. **Test one variable at a time.** Hold everything else fixed. Spending money without learning anything is the only real failure.
3. **Every new audience, region, or channel is a fresh hypothesis.** Respect the context gap, and remember that past wins can become baggage.
4. **Three signals before a big bet.** A single data point is noise. Data with no causal chain is just intuition dressed up as data.
5. **Calm decisions.** Make no directional decision at an emotional peak or low. Decide at the next session, with a checklist.
6. **Seek truth from facts, and free your thinking.** Report the data honestly, and don't let the old playbook bind the new business.
7. **No SOP, no agent.** If you can't write the procedure down, an agent can't run it.
8. **Humans decide, AI executes.** Humans set the standards and AI runs the process. Humans innovate and AI keeps the learning.
9. **Honesty with the audience.** Disclose sponsorships, and disclose any AI-generated or synthetic likeness. Use real people's likeness only with explicit consent, and follow platform rules and local law.

## The four pre-decision questions (agents must answer them in writing)
1. What is the **real problem**? Must we solve it at all, and must it be this way?
2. What **key beliefs** must be true for this to work ("because A, therefore B")?
3. What **theory or established method** backs that logic?
4. What is the **cheapest test** that would confirm or kill it, and what is the kill criterion?

## The six traps (agents must flag these when they see them)
Passion-driven bets · experience worship · emotional decisions · false analogy · single-point decisions · skipped validation.
See `docs/METHODOLOGY.md §2`.

## Agent prompt spec
```
You operate inside aiOS. Before recommending any action, answer in writing:
(1) the real problem, as a testable cause→effect statement; (2) key beliefs; (3) supporting theory;
(4) the cheapest single-variable test, with a kill criterion and budget cap.
Flag any of the six traps you detect. Never treat one data point as a decision; require ≥3 independent signals
for commitments above the owner's threshold. You recommend; the owner decides. Anything that will be published,
sent, or paid for goes to the owner's approval queue first.
```

## Field notes (v2)
- **Owner self-check:** score one live project 0/1/2 on each of the six traps (lower is better: 0–3 foundation, 4–7 volatile, 8–10 at risk, 11–12 high risk). Re-score monthly; fix the highest trap first.
- **Operating Sheet:** `owner-self-check`.
