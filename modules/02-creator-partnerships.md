# 02 · Creator partnerships

**Purpose:** find, reach, and keep the creators who validate your offer, show you the content structure that sells, and produce assets you can amplify.
**Remember:** the goal of the first creator wave is **learning and assets**, not short-term sales. The money comes later, from amplifying what works.

## Inputs
Offer brief and selling points (module 01), target audience segments, sample budget, commission policy, and the creator tools or exports you have access to.

## Procedure
1. **Define creator profiles per segment** (for example parents, fitness, beauty, home, general). For each one, write what their audience needs that the offer solves.
2. **Source candidates.** Score each on audience fit, content quality, engagement authenticity (flag inflated numbers), recent sales or affiliate history, and category overlap. Log them in `templates/creator-pipeline.csv`.
3. **Draft segment-specific outreach** with `templates/outreach.md`. It has three beats: *why you* (tied to their audience), *what's in it for you* (sample, commission, creative support, ad boosting), and *one clear next step*. Keep it short and easy to read in a crowded inbox. Write the subject line so it gets opened, and the first line so it doesn't read as cold.
4. **Queue outreach for owner approval** in Content Review (channel = `email`/`dm`, pillar = `creator-outreach`). Send only what's approved, and follow platform rules and anti-spam law.
5. **Climb the relationship ladder:** message → voice → a short call → product trial → commercial terms. Log every touch.
6. **Seed in small waves** (for example 20 samples). After each wave, run the diagnosis below before sending more. Lecture benchmark: a sample costs ≈$14 landed, so 100 samples ≈$1,400, and typically only 5–8 of 100 creators' videos sell. That loses money on paper and is still far cheaper than pre-stocking 2,000 units, because it buys validation and the content structure.
7. **Give each committed creator a brief** (`templates/creator-brief.md`): one selling point, the proven hook ideas, the Stop → Believe → Act structure, and disclosure requirements.
8. **Nurture top performers like distribution partners:** personal, thoughtful gestures, early access to new products, and co-created scripts. One long-term creator becomes a distributor for every future launch.

## Diagnosis when replies or returns are low (check in order)
1. Wrong creator profile? → 2. Weak opening line or subject? → 3. Weak reason to partner? → 4. Commission too low for the price point? (A 20% commission on a $30 product is about $6, rarely enough on its own.) → 5. Product differentiation too weak?

## Metrics and rules
- Reply rate, by segment and by template variant (A/B test one variable at a time)
- % moved to a direct contact channel
- **Content return rate on samples: target ≥ 90%.** Below that, pause and fix selection or terms.
- Videos to orders, and revenue per creator
- The owner watches outcome metrics, not activity counts.

## Human gate
Approve the outreach templates and any message sent under the owner's name, the sample budget, and commission or terms.

## Agent prompt spec
```
Role: Creator Partnerships Agent (aiOS module 02).
Given the offer brief, segments, and budget: (a) produce a creator-fit report per segment covering ideal profiles,
audience match, inflated-metrics risk, and a priority list; (b) write segment-specific outreach (subject,
first line that lowers the cold feel, benefit to them, sample/commission/support, one-step CTA; ≤120 words as an aiOS default);
(c) after each wave, diagnose low replies or returns in the fixed order and propose ONE change for the next wave.
Treat creators as long-term partners. Never send anything without owner approval. Always include disclosure guidance.
```

## Field notes (v2)
- Locate the funnel break first: no reply → no sample → no video → no sales. Each is a different fix.
- Communication ladder (strongest first): in person/gifts → group channel → call → voice → text.
- Owner metrics: reply rate, creator retention, video return rate (≈90%), re-booking/conversion rate (≈half of active creators), sales.
- **Operating Sheet:** `creator-pipeline` (KPIs: reply rate, content return rate, revenue per sample).
- Hire for communication, not platform know-how (lecture: CC hires experienced trade/export salespeople, who learn the platform from an SOP in half a day). Local students in the target market who can meet key creators in person are a cheap, high-trust channel.
- Two ladders, don't merge them: the **market** matures (creator-led → content+paid → live+brand → owned audience) while your **creator program** matures (cold start → growth → competitive → relationship assets). Market Stage sheet tracks both.
