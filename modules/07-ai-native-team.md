# 07 · AI-native team

**Purpose:** turn roles into SOPs and SOPs into agents, so capability belongs to the company, not to individuals.

## Principles
- Design every workflow with AI as the starting point, from the first hire and the first process.
- AI needs explicit knowledge. Turn tacit know-how into written manuals.
- Humans decide, set standards, and innovate. Agents execute, run the process, and keep the learning.

## Role manual (one per role; also the agent's system prompt)
`templates/role-manual.md` covers: 1) the company and its offers → 2) priority offers → 3) proven selling points → 4) target customer and creator profiles → 5) which scripts, outreach, and campaigns produced which results → 6) the SOP steps → 7) the metrics the role owns → 8) what needs human approval.

## Maturity ladder
| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| L1 | Repetitive frontline tasks automated (research, first drafts, report reading) |
| L2 | Managers orchestrate agents across a function |
| L3 | Cross-functional agent workflows (insight → script → asset → paid diagnosis) |
| L4 | The leader runs the operation through role agents ("How's the business this week?") |

## Staged adoption
- **0→1:** automate only what produces positive feedback fast (for example batch content drafts, review mining).
- **1→10:** convert one role at a time, starting with the most repetitive.
- **10→100:** reuse the agents across markets and categories.
Don't paste a "perfect AI org" onto a real company. Upgrade it one workflow at a time.

## Suggested agent roster (all report into one hub and one approval queue)
Operator (status and priorities) · Opportunity Analyst (01) · Creator Partnerships (02) · Content Engine (03) · Paid Analyst (04) · Measurement (05) · Finance check (unit economics and spend vs. targets).

## Metric
Revenue per person (people + agents). Lecture benchmarks: ≈$0.86M per person per year for a good e-commerce team, ≈$1.1–1.4M with AI, ≈$0.3–0.4M in bloated teams (converted at ~7:1). Hire for the size of business a person plus agents can drive.

## Field notes (v2)
- AI owns: market insight, selection analysis, creator outreach, video breakdown, ad diagnosis, data review. Humans own: problem definition, standards, process design, judging results, training the system.
- Orchestrator pattern: one lead agent decomposes a goal, dispatches specialist agents, and returns one plan for human approval.
- **Operating Sheet:** `role-kpis`.
