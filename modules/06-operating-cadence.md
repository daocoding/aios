# 06 · Operating cadence

**Purpose:** the rhythm that ties the modules together. An agent can schedule all of it with any routine or cron system.

| When | Ritual | Owner time | Agent work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily, morning | **1-minute stand-up per producer** | 5 min | Prepare yesterday's asset metrics, anomalies, and why the winners won |
| Daily | **Approval pass** in Content Review | 5–10 min | Queue drafts with hypotheses; owner bulk-approves rows |
| Mon | **Positioning meeting** | 20 min | Propose this week's two selling points and frameworks |
| Tue / Thu | **Paid review** | 10 min | Module 04 diagnosis |
| Wed | **Creator wave review** | 10 min | Module 02 diagnosis; next wave |
| Fri | **Weekly review** | 30 min | Module 05 compile; SOP/prompt diffs |
| Day 30 | **Sprint retrospective** | 60 min | Scale, iterate, or stop; next launch plan |
| Quarterly | **Opportunity scan** | 60 min | Module 01 on the current line and new candidates |

**Calm-decision rule:** if a meeting gets heated over a directional choice, the agent records the options and puts the decision on the next session's agenda with a checklist.

## The 30-day test sprint (default template)
- **Days 1–3:** launch plan approved; small batch in place; hook library seeded with 20+ references; first creator wave out.
- **Days 4–10:** selling points 1–2 tested through own content and creators; daily stand-ups.
- **Days 11–17:** selling points 3–4; first paid tests in 0→1 mode (aim for ~50 conversions).
- **Days 18–24:** double down on the leading selling point; remake heroes; second creator wave.
- **Days 25–30:** read out the three signals; retrospective; decide whether to scale, iterate, or stop.

## Field notes (v2)
- Org path: 0→1 founder judgment, 1→10 module coordination, 10→100 organizational system.
